Taco Noodle Bowl

End-of-month survival cooking at its finest

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🍳 Cook: 20 min 👥 Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 packet taco mince (or any ground meat)
  • 200g noodles of your choice
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 2 tbsp gochujang sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Spring onions & olives to top

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles according to packet. Drain and set aside.
  2. Brown the mince in a pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add taco seasoning, soy sauce, and gochujang. Stir well.
  4. Toss noodles through the mince mixture.
  5. Serve topped with spring onions and olives.
💡 Tip: Works great as a freezer meal — freeze the mince mixture separately and cook fresh noodles when needed.
