Café Review January 24, 2026

Gather Grounds Coffee Shop, Phnom Penh

If you live in Phnom Penh long enough, you start judging coffee shops on very specific criteria: Can I sit here for more than 30 minutes? Is the coffee actually good? Will anyone side-eye me for opening my laptop? Gather Grounds quietly ticks all those boxes. It's calm without being boring, and the coffee is solid and dependable — the kind that does its job without demanding attention. A genuinely good spot for tired teachers, remote workers, and anyone who just needs to breathe.
