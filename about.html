🐥 About Us

Two chicks, one city, and a whole lot of iced coffee.

Who Are We?

We're Azel and Douwlene — two South African women who packed up their lives and landed in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We're teachers, tutors, business builders, and proud dog moms (hi Harley and Quinn 🐾) with one very opinionated cat named Zeus.

TFC Living Life started as a place to be honest about what expat life actually looks like — not the highlight reel, but the real stuff. The end-of-month cooking when the budget is tight. The café that saved a terrible Tuesday. The freezer meal that was surprisingly incredible. The moments that make you laugh at your own life.

We live it, we eat it, we write about it. That's the whole deal.

What's This Blog About?

Three things: real recipes, honest café reviews, and expat life in Cambodia.

We don't do perfect. We do practical. Budget meals that actually taste good. Coffee shops you can actually work in. End-of-month wins when payday feels far away. And the occasional moment of "how is this our life?" that makes you grateful for all of it.

If you're an expat, a tired person who still wants to eat well, or someone who just loves a good café find — welcome home. 🌴
